In an oppressivly pink world, Punk Rock Puppy is a dime a dozen. Most doggies spend their time screaming into mics, slapping their basses, or banging their drums, so hows a dog supposed to stand out? Punk Rock Puppy wants more than to blend into the crowd, much to the chagrin of their parents. Surrounded by battle vests and patch pants, Punk rock puppy finds themself drawn to the frillier things in life. Late one night searching the dark web they found something new, enticing, and strange: Bach.

How will their family react when Punk Rock Puppy becomes... Mx. Classical??? Will Mx. Classical find an audience in their strict punk world? And most importantly, who's Bach?

All will be revealed, in due time.
